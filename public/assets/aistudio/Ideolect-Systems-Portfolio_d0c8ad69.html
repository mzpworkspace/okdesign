Studio vision

Keep human intent
in the work.

A designer sketches how a feature should feel. A researcher asks who should be able to use it. An engineer has to show that the code does what both intended.

Ideolect’s vision is to keep those decisions visible. PenSuite gives the design a place to take shape. Pencore asks what the evidence supports. Cortex 7.2 asks whether the implementation survives a test.
